What the regional sliders mean

Official 2024 parliamentary election results provide a separate voting pattern for every municipality. The simulator aggregates these onto its 42-comarca map and combines them with 2025 Idescat municipal population figures. This is a model of electoral geography, not a measurement of each resident’s political beliefs.

In the simulator: choose Regional evidence → 2024 votes + population. Select a comarca or municipality, then open Regional profile & biases to see its estimated slider fit and observed 2024 vote shares. Party editors offer an editable tag, a 2024 geography reference, and Use suggested sliders. Historical mode applies referenced voting geography. Ideological mode ignores scenario party identities and references; all parties compete through their five sliders and national vote targets. Regional sensitivity controls how strongly local preferences matter.

Evidence and interpretation

Small municipalities should not automatically be coded as socially conservative or economically right-wing. In the CEO’s April 2024 survey, left–right self-placement was similar across municipal-size groups. Support for independence differed more: 56.5% in municipalities below 10,000 residents, 24.8% in the 150,001–1,000,000 group, and 43.0% in Barcelona’s size group. Those are broad survey groups, not estimates for individual municipalities. The survey has 1,500 respondents and does not justify 947 separate local ideology measurements. CEO electoral survey 2024, tables 7 and 11, pages 32–33.

That distinction matters in Osona and Ripollès: a strong independentist vote does not tell us whether voters prefer ERC, Junts, CUP or Aliança’s very different economic and social policies. Barcelona and its surrounding municipalities also differ substantially. Local party vote shares remain the main source of those differences; the averaged slider scores are a compact reference.

Definitions and limits

  • Social: 0 conservative → 100 progressive. Economic: 0 free market → 100 state intervention. These are separate assumed party-position scales; neither is a direct conversion of CEO left–right self-placement.
  • National position: 0 unionist → 100 independentist. Intermediate values represent modeled federalist or self-determination positions. This weighted score is not the percentage who support independence.
  • Rural / metropolitan: municipality-size proxy: ≤2,000 → 0; ≤10,000 → 20; ≤50,000 → 40; ≤150,000 → 60; ≤1,000,000 → 80; larger → 100. This does not measure population density, metropolitan membership, or rural land use. Comarca/province scores are population-weighted.
  • Organization: 0 established → 100 populist. This is the least empirically grounded scale. Its ideological-distance weight is 5%, versus 34% national position, 22% social, 22% economic and 17% geography.
  • Social, economic, organization and national-position scores are weighted by the ten modeled parties’ 2024 candidature votes. Remaining parties are excluded from these averages; coverage is shown. Scores are rounded to five in the UI to avoid implying precise measurement.
  • Modern municipal results are aggregated using the existing map’s historical membership; Lluçanès remains within Osona. These aggregates are not the official 2024 comarca boundary system. Medinyà has no separate 2024 result and retains the older fallback.
  • Changing evidence preserves scenario turnout and national vote targets through calibration. It changes geographic vote patterns, not the selected election’s total turnout. In Ideological mode, municipal votes (plus overseas ballots) are calibrated together and summed into comarca and provincial totals. Manual local overrides are applied afterward and can change national shares. Historical mode retains its reference-based calculations.
  • In Ideological mode, each municipality retains a mixture of estimated voter profiles inferred from observed vote shares. Support is the weighted sum of proximity to those profiles, rather than proximity to their average. A squared distance across the five axes is converted to affinity using an exponential curve, then calibrated to national targets. Names, IDs and scenario party references do not enter this calculation. Sensitivity 0 removes regional ideological differences; 70 is the default; 100 strengthens them. These are simulation assumptions, not measured voter probabilities.

Sources and reproducibility

Generalitat · definitive 2024 election dataset supplies municipal totals and provincial results; overseas records are excluded from municipal aggregations. Idescat population API supplies 1 January 2025 municipal population. The generator stores these separately from the 2015 electoral baseline.

Policy references: PSC 2024 program; ERC 2024 program; Comuns programs; CUP political principles; Aliança 2024 program; Vox 2024 fiscal proposals. These sources support broad policy differences; the particular 0–100 values, especially organization, remain editable estimates.

The local generator is scripts/catalonia-regional-profiles.py. Its input snapshots are in research/catalonia. Run it to reproduce catalonia-regional-profiles.js and its public mirror.
